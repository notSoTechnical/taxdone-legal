Tax Done AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Tax Done AI ("the app") is a tax organization and estimation tool for individuals, freelancers, and small businesses, published by Venturebnb LLC. Contact: info@annizon.com.

What the app does with your data

Your financial records stay on your device. In the current version, receipts, bank statement imports, transactions, mileage logs, and estimates are stored locally on your phone. We do not operate a server that receives your financial records, and we never sell personal or financial data — ever.

Accounts

Account sign-in is being rolled out. When accounts become available, synced data will be encrypted in transit and at rest, you will be able to export everything for free at any time, and you will be able to delete your account and its data from inside the app. This policy will be updated before accounts go live.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive subscription status (such as whether a plan is active), never your payment card details.

What we collect

The app does not collect analytics about your financial records. If crash reporting is enabled in a future version, it will contain technical diagnostics only, never receipt images, transactions, or amounts.

Exports

Anything you export (PDF, CSV) is created on your device and shared only where you send it.

Important limits

Tax Done AI organizes records and estimates taxes. It is not tax, legal, or accounting advice, and it does not prepare or file tax returns.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account & data wipes all local records. Questions or deletion requests: info@annizon.com.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.
